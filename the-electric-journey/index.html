The Electric Journey

A cinematic family adventure exploring electric mobility and breathtaking landscapes in Brazil.

The transition to electric mobility is not only a technological shift, it is a cultural and emotional transformation.

The Journey

The Beginning: preparing for a new kind
of journey

1

The story starts before departure. Can an electric vehicle support a real family adventure? What changes when your roadtrip starts with charging instead of fueling? How does a family prepare for traveling with two small children?

The Adventure: discovering Brazil
electrically

2

The road becomes the main character. Hotels, landscapes, local culture, hidden destinations and have candid family moments.

The Challenges: real EV ownership

3

Authenticity creates trust. The journey explores charging stops, planning routes, range expectations, unexpected situations and learning curve of EV ownership. Not an advertisement, a real experience.

The Connection: Brazil meets China

4

The family represents a cultural bridge. The story explores Chinese innovation, Brazilian lifestyle and global technology adapting to local realities.

Ongoing Reflection: The future of family
mobility

5

After thousands of kilometers, the question becomes: “How will families travel in the next generation?”
